Why I built it

I'm exploring ways to increase the communication bandwidth between me and my AI agents.

1

Typing is slow

When I had to type every request to an agent, I kept my explanations short and just typed out specific instructions instead of the whole context.

2

Talking is three times faster

Dictation let me explain the problem I was trying to solve, with my reasoning behind it. It allowed me to increase the amount of intent I shared with my agents, and the results I got from them got much closer to what I had in mind.

3

Increasing the bandwidth with our agents

How much intent can we convey to our agents and how much understanding can we get back from them? Reading a huge wall of text back is slow. What if I could have the agent explain it to me live, using its voice and showing me what it means?

4

So I built Command Bridge

It's a CLI tool and local voice models that let my agents transcribe my speech, and, using just command-line tools, draw, point at things and talk to me.

Local

Speech recognition, the voice and the voiceprint run on your machine. No account and no speech API. A CPU is enough, and a GPU makes it faster.

Any agent

The tool holds no model. If your agent can run a command, it can talk to you. Claude Code, Codex and Grok have each driven it unchanged.

One command to learn it

command-bridge describe hands your agent the whole contract, so it sets itself up and knows how to listen.
